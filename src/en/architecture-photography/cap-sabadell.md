## Healthcare Architecture Photography: CAP Sabadell by EMBA

This institutional architectural photography series documents the innovative design of the **Sabadell Primary Healthcare Centre in** **Sabadell, Barcelona**. Designed by the acclaimed studio **[EMBA, Estudi Massip-Bosch Arquitectes](https://www.emba.cat)**, this public medical facility stands as a masterclass in civic retrofitting, showing how modern medical facilities can expand within a sensitive heritage context.

The photoshoot documents the clean functional lines and open spatial geometries, with a fluid architectural dialogue between heritage structures and clinical environments.

### Protecting History while Modernising Public Healthcare

The visual narrative of this institutional architecture shoot highlights EMBA’s strategic blending of old and new architectural styles.

* **The Central Integration Courtyard:** A defining feature of the design is a central open-air courtyard that connects different eras. The photography captures a historic masonry building beside a contemporary clinic exterior with sleek, horizontal metal panelling and large glass openings. This contrast shows how the public space breathes, connecting the city’s industrial past with its modern public works infrastructure.
* **Luminous, Multi-Level Interiors:** Inside the facility, the architectural focus turns to vertical depth and natural light. The compositions frame a bright, double-height clinic interior anchored by a minimalist architectural staircase. Floor-to-ceiling windows fill the vertical circulation core with soft daylight, turning a public clinical space into an open, inviting environment.

### Technical Precision in Clinical Interior Photography

Documenting public medical facilities requires a disciplined photographic approach that prioritises cleanliness, geometric accuracy, and spatial clarity.

The interior sequences frame long clinical hallways lined with crisp, white integrated cabinetry. The compositions use open examination room doors to capture cascading light and deep layers of reflection across the pristine, light-colored floors. Strict **orthogonal alignment** maintains the horizontal facade panels and inner geometric framing in accordance with the architect’s specifications. This portfolio piece provides a high-fidelity visual archive of functional, empathetic healthcare design in Catalonia.
