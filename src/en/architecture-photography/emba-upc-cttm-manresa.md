## University Architecture Photography: UPC CTTM Manresa by EMBA

This collection of institutional architectural photography highlights the striking, forward-looking design of the **Centre de Transferència Tecnològica de Manresa (CTTM)**, a specialised technological research facility at the **Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC)** campus in **Manresa**.

Designed by the acclaimed studio **[EMBA, Estudi Massip-Bosch Arquitectes](https://www.emba.cat)**, this academic infrastructure project balances industrial efficiency with advanced educational spatial design. The photoshoot shows how rigorous geometric volumes shape a modern environment optimised for higher education, engineering research, and technical collaboration.

### Documenting Technical Laboratories and Industrial Materiality

The visual narrative of this university portfolio focuses on clean structural lines, functional transparency, and the honest expression of building materials, continuing the project’s emphasis on clarity and precision.

* **Sleek Industrial Aesthetics:** EMBA’s design embraces raw, engineered materials suited to a technological transport and maintenance centre. The photography emphasises the smooth textures of exposed concrete, industrial steel frames, and extensive glass panels, while capturing a clean architectural style.
* **Luminous Research Spaces:** Inside the facility, the photography focuses on spatial volume and illumination tracking. The compositions frame technical laboratories and academic workshops, showing how massive windows fill deep interior spaces with uniform, diffuse natural light—ideal for accurate engineering and research.
* **Symmetrical Corridors and Circulation:** The indoor framing uses deep leading lines to capture the building’s circulation routes. Pristine hallways, framed by minimalist partition grids and polished flooring systems, demonstrate the smooth, logical flow designed to handle daily traffic from students, professors, and researchers.

### A Professional Technical Approach to Academic Portfolios

Photographing complex university campuses and advanced technical facilities requires absolute geometric precision. Every photograph in this architectural gallery features strict **orthogonal alignment**, keeping the tall structural columns, heavy window frames, and wall boundaries perfectly vertical to honour the architects’ technical blueprints.

By balancing changing daylight shining through the main glass facades with the building’s internal industrial lighting, this portfolio offers a polished, high-fidelity visual record of EMBA’s mastery of contemporary institutional architecture in Catalonia.
