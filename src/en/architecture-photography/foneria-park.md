## Landscape Architecture Photography: Foneria Park

This outdoor architectural portfolio documents **Foneria Park**, a carefully designed public green space in **Sant Vicenç dels Horts, Barcelona**. Landscape architect [Isidre Santacreu](https://www.santacreu-moliner.com) designed the project, which highlights the role of modern civil infrastructure in creating community-focused urban ecosystems.

The photoshoot captures the flowing balance between natural landscaping, paved pedestrian walkways, and functional architectural installations.

### Capturing Urban Green Areas and Structural Elements

The visual narrative of this landscape architecture photography portfolio centres on spatial layout, material interplay, and environmental balance within public parks.

* **Symmetrical Paved Walkways:** The park’s layout relies on sweeping, paved walkways that cut clean lines through grassy lawns and young tree groves. The photography uses wide-angle frames and strong leading lines to trace these routes, showing how the pathways direct movement through the park.
* **Geometric Trellises and Green Walls:** A prominent design feature is the installation of minimalist **metal trellises** supporting climbing plants. The compositions frame these grids against the sky, showing how steel frames can blend with organic greenery to provide shade and privacy.
* **Concrete and Metal Public Infrastructure:** The photoshoot highlights a central public pavilion with a concrete structure, heavy industrial railings, and vibrant **yellow accent walls**. The photographs use bold angles to emphasise this structural volume, contrasting hard industrial materials with the soft textures of nearby park benches and vegetation.

### Technical Precision in Outdoor Architectural Shoots

Photographing expansive public parks and landscape architecture entails careful planning around daylight and vanishing points.

To respect Isidre Santacreu’s civil design blueprint, the photographs are composed with strict vertical alignment, keeping metal pillars, staircases, and structural railings upright. Exposure is calibrated to balance the open sky with the textures of grass, paved stone, and concrete volumes, creating a high-contrast yet detailed outcome. This series provides a professional visual archive that highlights how contemporary landscape design revitalises suburban community spaces in Catalonia.
