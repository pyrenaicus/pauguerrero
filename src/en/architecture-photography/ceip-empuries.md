## Educational Architecture Photography: CEIP Empúries by EMBA

This institutional architectural photography collection documents **CEIP Empúries**, a public primary and nursery school designed by the forward-thinking studio **[EMBA, Estudi Massip-Bosch Arquitectes](https://www.emba.cat)**.

The photoshoot shows how public architecture creates safe, inspiring, and dynamic spaces built to foster learning and community interaction.

### Documenting Playful Volumetric Geometry and Adaptive Classrooms

The visual narrative of this elementary school photoshoot highlights structural scale, material clarity, and the fluid integration of indoor and outdoor spaces.

* **Volumetric Forms and Presence:** EMBA’s design relies on bold, geometric volumes that give the institution a strong identity. The exterior photography captures these crisp volumetric shifts, utilising sharp horizontal and wide-angle perspective frames to show how the school sits in balance with its surrounding environment.
* **Luminous, Open Learning Environments:** Inside the school, the photography emphasises spatial openness and the distribution of light. The compositions feature double-height communal halls and airy classrooms, where deep-set windows let natural light fill the interiors. The exposures are metered to balance bright outdoor play areas with the indoor desks, creating a design that eliminates harsh shadows and preserves a focused, cheerful academic mood.
* **Child-Centric Functional Design:** The images document the fluid pathways connecting the learning rooms to the outdoor courtyards. The framing highlights durable, modern materials alongside clean partition grids, capturing an architecture that accommodates the high-energy demands of daily student life while maintaining architectural restraint.

### Technical Precision in Public Educational Portfolios

Photographing modern educational institutions requires a disciplined approach to geometry and lens selection. Every photograph in this architectural gallery maintains strict **orthogonal alignment**, assuring that structural pillars, window frames, and wall divisions remain vertical and true to the architect’s blueprint.

By balancing changing daylight flowing through the main windows with the building’s internal lighting setups, this portfolio offers a polished, high-fidelity archive of EMBA’s mastery of contemporary public works and educational spatial design in Catalonia.
