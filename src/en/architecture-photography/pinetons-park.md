## Landscape Architecture Photography: Pinetons Park by Isidre Santacreu

This landscape architecture portfolio piece documents **Pinetons Park** (*Parc dels Pinetons*), an 11-hectare green space in **Ripollet, Barcelona**. Designed by landscape architect **[Isidre Santacreu](https://www.santacreu-moliner.com)**, the park creates a gradual transition between the expanding urban residential zones and the natural Mediterranean landscape.

The photoshoot captures the large-scale geometric layouts, modern shade structures, and central focal points that characterise this open-air community sanctuary.

### Documenting Civic Interventions: Steel, Wood, and Water

The visual storytelling of this public park photoshoot highlights structural linearity, functional public spaces, and natural scenery.

* **Modern Shade Architectures:** A central visual anchor of the open plazas is two custom-designed, contemporary steel-and-wood **shade structures**. The photography uses low- and wide-angle frames to emphasise the geometry of these minimalist pergolas, showing how their timber slats and iron frames cast shadows throughout the stone walkways.
* **The Central Water Jet Feature:** The series documents the park’s iconic landmark—a massive public fountain with a powerful **vertical water jet**. The compositions capture this feature from multiple angles, showing how the plume serves as a landmark visible from the surrounding town.
* **Topographical and Linear Rhythms:** Rows of stone steps, gravel walkways, and winter trees carve clean paths through the landscape. The photographs frame these features as leading lines, showing the drainage and grading systems that handle rainwater and organise visitor movement.

### Technical Balance in Open-Air Portfolio Shoots

Photographing sprawling public landscapes requires navigating vast horizons and unpredictable natural light. Every frame in this collection uses careful horizontal and vertical perspective lines to prevent wide-angle lens distortion across the steel frames and stairways.

By precisely balancing exposures to capture both the bright sky and the textures of the stone steps, lawns, and woodwork, this architectural photography series provides a permanent, professional visual archive of urban renaturalization in Catalonia.
