## Educational Architecture Photography: IES Fontajau by EMBA

This architectural photography showcase documents the modern structure of **IES Fontajau**, a public secondary high school in Girona’s Fontajau neighbourhood. Designed by **[EMBA, Estudi Massip-Bosch Arquitectes](https://www.emba.cat)**, led by Enric Massip-Bosch, this educational building is a masterful study in contemporary institutional design.

The photoshoot focuses on how public architecture can foster an inspiring learning environment through clean structural geometries, vast windows, and integrated community pathways.

### Capturing Sleek Exterior Geometries and Urban Integration

The exterior architecture photography emphasises the building’s striking volumetric presence and its relation to Girona’s public landscape.

* **Sleek Minimalist Facades:** The compositions leverage sharp horizontal and vertical perspective lines, framing the crisp exterior panels and massive glass openings in clean daylight.
* **Contextual Framing:** Rather than isolating the building, the images place the school’s facade with trees and street-level details such as parked motorcycles. This grounds the structure in its active community context.

### Documenting Luminous and Functional Educational Interiors

Photographing interior institutional spaces demands balancing spatial volume with the functional materials used in daily student life.

* **The Volumetric Gymnasium:** A key sequence captures the expansive school gymnasium. Wide-angle frames emphasise the double-height open floor plan and vibrant multi-sport flooring. A grid of windows anchors the composition and shows how the design fills the athletic space with soft, diffuse light.
* **The Modern Classroom Environment:** Inside the classrooms, the photography shifts to a calm, contemplative mood. The frames capture rows of wooden desks and chairs, with large blackboards in the background. Carefully metered exposures show how natural light enters through deep-set windows, reducing harsh shadows and preserving a focused academic environment.

This architectural portfolio piece provides a visual archive of public works excellence and innovative educational spatial planning in Catalonia.
