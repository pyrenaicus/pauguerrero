I value your privacy and believe in keeping things transparent. This website is designed to collect as little data as possible. Below is an explanation of exactly what happens to your information when you visit this site.

## 1\. Data Controller

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable local data protection laws (such as Spain's LOPDGDD), the data controller is:

* **Name:** Pau Guerrero
* **Location:** Barcelona, Spain
* **Website:** [pauguerrero.com](https://pauguerrero.com/)
* **Contact Email:** info@pauguerrero.com

## 2\. Information Collected and Why

This website does not require you to create an account, log in, or accept invasive tracking mechanisms. There is only one way personal data is collected:

### The Contact Form

If you choose to reach out via the contact form, the site collects the information you explicitly type into the fields:

* **Name:** To know how to address you.
* **Email Address:** To reply to your inquiry.
* **Message Content:** To understand the context of your message.

**Legal Basis for Processing:** The processing of this data is based entirely on your **consent** when you actively click the "Send" (or equivalent) button to transmit your message.

## 3\. Web Analytics (Privacy-First)

To understand how many people visit this site and which pages are popular, I use **GoatCounter**, a privacy-focused web analytics service.

* GoatCounter **does not track individual users**.
* It **does not use cookies**.
* It **does not collect personally identifiable information (PII)** like your full IP address, location, or browsing history across other sites.
* All data is aggregated, meaning I only see general metrics (e.g., "10 people visited the home page today").

You can read more about GoatCounter's strict approach to privacy in the [GoatCounter Privacy Policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/privacy).

## 4\. Data Sharing and Third Parties

I do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third-party companies for marketing purposes.

Your contact form messages are processed strictly to handle your request. Depending on your hosting/infrastructure setup, your message may pass through an automated email routing service or form handling utility solely to deliver the email to my inbox.

## 5\. Data Retention

I only keep the information submitted through the contact form for as long as it is necessary to fulfill your request, maintain our professional communication, or comply with standard legal record-keeping obligations. If our conversation concludes and there is no ongoing business relationship, your emails are periodically deleted.

## 6\. Your Rights Under GDPR

Because this data processing falls under EU jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

* **Right to Access:** You can ask for a copy of the data I hold about you.
* **Right to Rectification:** You can ask to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
* **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You can request that I completely delete the messages and contact info you sent me.
* **Right to Withdraw Consent:** You can withdraw your consent to data processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, simply send an email to info@pauguerrero.com, and I will handle your request promptly.
