**Pau Guerrero** is an architect and photographer specialising in architectural, luxury retail, and fine art landscape photography. His architectural photography practice focuses on revealing the dialogue between form and function and highlighting the sensory qualities and physical materiality of built environments. Working alongside international brands, architecture studios, interior designers, and luxury boutiques, he captures the meticulous execution of premium retail design. Pau's work has been featured in numerous solo and group exhibitions, and his fine art photography is represented in private and public collections, including the [Fundació Vila Casas](https://www.fundaciovilacasas.com/es/obras/pau-guerrero).
